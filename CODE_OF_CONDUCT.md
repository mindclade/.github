# Mindclade Code of Conduct

Mindclade contributors, maintainers, and participants must treat one another with professionalism, respect, and care. Harassment, discrimination, threats, retaliation, and disclosure of private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

Keep reviews focused on the work, assume good intent, and raise safety or security concerns through the appropriate private route. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect participants and the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to <engineering@mindclade.com>. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
