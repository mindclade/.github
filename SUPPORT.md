# Support

For contribution and source questions, open the appropriate issue template after reading [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

For security-sensitive matters, do not open a public issue. Contact engineering@mindclade.com as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This repository is pre-production. Requests to connect workflows or change live organization settings must follow the governance and activation boundary in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
